Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Brainy Bits ("we") runs the YouTube channel @factsbrainybits and an internal tool, Brainy Bits Factory, that uploads our own videos to that channel with the YouTube API Services.

What the tool does

Brainy Bits Factory is used only by the channel owner. It uploads videos we create to our own YouTube channel, using Google OAuth with the youtube.upload permission. It has no public sign-in and no other users.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data about viewers or any other YouTube users. The tool does not read YouTube user data. The only data it handles is the OAuth credential for our own channel, which is kept as an encrypted secret in our private build environment, and the ID of each video we upload.

YouTube and Google

Brainy Bits Factory uses YouTube API Services. By watching our videos on YouTube you are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. The channel owner can revoke the tool's access at any time from Google security settings.
